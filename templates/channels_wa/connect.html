Where do I find these?

  1. Open business.facebook.com and go to WhatsApp Manager.
  2. Choose your number, then open the API Setup page.
  3. The Phone number ID and WhatsApp Business Account ID are both on that page.
  4. The access token comes from Business Settings → System users → Generate token (choose a permanent one).
  5. The app secret is in your app's dashboard under Settings → Basic.

Nothing is sent to WhatsApp until you press Check connection on the next screen.
